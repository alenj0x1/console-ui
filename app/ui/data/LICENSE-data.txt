Suggestion data in this folder, built by tools/build_language_data.py:

words-<lang>.txt
  Derived from FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave
  (https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords, commit 525f9b560de45753a5ea01069454e72e9aa541c6),
  word counts from the OpenSubtitles 2018 corpus (http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles2018.php).
  License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). These derived lists are shared under the same license.
  Changes: top 50,000 words per language filtered to plain words; unaccented spellings rarer than
  10 % of their accented twin removed.

pairs-<lang>.tsv
  Word-pair counts computed from Tatoeba sentences (https://tatoeba.org), export of 2026-10-31.
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/).
  Changes: lowercased, tokenized into words, pairs seen at least 2 times, top 200 per word kept.

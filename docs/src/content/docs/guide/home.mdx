**Home** is Console UI's full-screen home screen. Hold <Btn b="Menu" /> to open it from anywhere, and hold it again (or press <Btn b="B" />) to go back to the pointer. Opening the app, or clicking its tray icon, also shows it.

It has four tabs, which <Btn b="LB" /> and <Btn b="RB" /> switch between: **Games**, **Apps**, **Media** and **Settings** (see [Settings](../settings/)). The focused item shows its details and its main action above the row of tiles; the hints next to it show the buttons that do the rest.

## Games

Your installed Steam and Epic Games games appear by themselves, with Steam's official art as the background of the focused one.

- <Btn b="A" /> **Play** starts the game, and the controller passes to it ([game mode](../game-mode/)).
- **Resume.** Games that already have a window open come first, the last one you played before the others, and their button says **Resume**: it brings the game back to the front instead of starting it again. The list follows games you open or close while Home is showing. With none open, the last game you played comes first.
- **Order.** After the open ones, games are sorted by most recent or A–Z (**Game order** in [Customize](../personalize/)).
- **Trailers.** After a couple of seconds on a Steam game, its trailer plays over the art, silent and in a loop. It is the first part of the game's main trailer, downloaded once from the Steam store and kept. <Btn b="X" /> turns its sound on or off, and that choice stays for every trailer after it (also **Trailers with sound** in Settings › Games).
- **Search.** <Btn b="Y" /> opens a search with letters and numbers you pick with the controller. Results are in alphabetical order and match the start of each word in the title; <Btn b="LT" /> and <Btn b="RT" /> jump to the previous or next letter.

## Apps

The apps you pin, with their real icons. The **Edit apps** tile lists everything in the Start menu (Store apps included) to pin or unpin, up to 24 apps. <Btn b="A" /> opens an app.

## Media

Your music and video apps and streaming sites in one row.

- At first it shows the media apps installed on your PC (Spotify, VLC and similar). **Edit Media** adds or removes apps (media apps first, every other app under **All apps**) and the sites YouTube, YouTube Music, Netflix, Twitch, Prime Video and Disney+, which open in your default browser, where you are already signed in.
- **What is playing comes first**, with the song on its card: whatever reports itself to Windows' media controls (the same ones the volume flyout shows), such as Spotify or a browser. With the focus on it, the background takes the colours of the cover and shows the song's progress.
- <Btn b="X" /> plays or pauses, <Btn b="LT" /> / <Btn b="RT" /> change the song.
- **High-resolution covers and lyrics.** The cover is looked up in high resolution, and synced lyrics show with the line being sung lit and centred. <Btn b="R3" /> on the playing card switches between the lyrics and the cover; the choice is kept (**Show song lyrics**). Both can be turned off: see [Privacy and security](../../how-it-works/privacy-security/#covers-and-lyrics-from-the-internet).

## Volume

On every tab, up and down on the d-pad change the system volume (in the **List** and **Grid** shapes they move through the tiles instead, and <Btn b="RT+updown" /> keeps the volume). <Btn b="Y" /> mutes, except in Games, where it searches.

## Shapes

**Home layout** (in Customize and in Settings › Appearance) changes how Games, Apps and Media look:

| Shape | Looks like |
|---|---|
| **Wide** | A row of wide banners (the default) |
| **Covers** | A row of box covers |
| **Grid** | A grid of covers |
| **List** | A list of names |

With big text or a long title, tiles give up some size so the details above them are never cut.

<Aside>
Something that can't be opened or saved shows a short notice on Home instead of failing silently.
</Aside>

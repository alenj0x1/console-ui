TV mode is for a PC connected to a monitor and a TV: it leaves only the TV on, as the main screen, and sends the sound to it. Turning it off puts the screens and the sound back as they were.

<Steps>

1. In **Settings › TV mode**, choose **TV screen** (the screen that stays on).

2. Optionally choose **Sound in TV mode**, the sound output to use, such as the TV's speakers. **No change** (the default) leaves the sound where it is.

3. Turn on **TV mode**. The app asks **Does the TV look right?** Press <Btn b="A" /> **Keep it** to keep it.

</Steps>

If you can't see the question because the TV shows nothing, just wait: with no answer in 15 seconds, everything goes back by itself. <Btn b="B" /> goes back right away.

## Turning it off

- Turn **TV mode** off in Settings, or
- open the [control center](../game-mode/#control-center), press <Btn b="Menu" /> and choose **Turn off TV mode**.

<Aside title="If the app closed while TV mode was on">
TV mode doesn't change the screen layout Windows keeps saved, so Windows + P (or a restart) brings the usual one back. Opening Console UI again also puts the screens and the sound back as they were.
</Aside>

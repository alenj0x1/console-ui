## Customize

On **Games**, **Apps** or **Media**, press <Btn b="Menu" /> to open **Customize**, a panel over Home with how it looks. Every change shows at once behind the panel; <Btn b="B" /> or <Btn b="Menu" /> closes it. A line under the options says what the focused one does.

| Option | What it changes |
|---|---|
| **Background** | The background behind Home (see below) |
| **Main color** | The colour of the selection frame and of buttons like **Play**: blue (the default), violet, green, amber, pink or ivory. Hidden in high contrast, which uses yellow |
| **Darken the background** | Darkens the background, to read better over busy pictures (0 to 100 %) |
| **Home layout** | Wide banners, box covers, a grid or a list ([shapes](../home/#shapes)) |
| **Game order** | **Recent** or **A–Z**; open games always come first |
| **Game pictures** | The focused game's art behind Games |
| **Text size** | Text size, from 100 to 150 % |
| **Show song lyrics** | Lyrics in place of the cover in Media |

The same options, and more, are in [Settings](../settings/).

## Backgrounds

**Background** opens a grid of backgrounds with a live preview of the focused one; <Btn b="A" /> **Use** picks it.

- **Built-in animations.** Five animated backgrounds drawn by the graphics card: **Diagonal wave** (the default), **Ripples**, **Rain**, **Breathing** and **Line waves**.
- **Wallpaper Engine.** If you have Wallpaper Engine, its video wallpapers (from the Workshop and your own projects) show up. Scene and web wallpapers need Wallpaper Engine itself to play, so they are left out. **Get more** opens its Workshop.
- **Your videos and pictures.** Those in your Videos and Pictures folders, plus any folder you add with **Add folder**, browsing your drives with the controller. Folders on a network share are not accepted.
- **No background.** The plain background colour.
- **Game art** turns the game art on Games on or off.

The background pauses while it can't be seen. With Windows' **Show animations** turned off, backgrounds and previews stay still and trailers don't play.
